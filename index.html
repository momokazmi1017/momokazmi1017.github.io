Liquid Rocket Engine Preliminary Design Tool

Turns thrust, chamber pressure and propellants into a complete preliminary thrust-chamber design. Used to design the E5-75, a 5 kN (1,124 lbf) LOX / 75% ethanol regen-cooled engine at 25 bar.

  • Equilibrium combustion (Cantera); ideal Isp within 0.6% of Sutton & Biblarz
  • Cooling trade study cut the hot wall from 1,124 K to 758 K, under the CuCrZr limit
  • Method-of-characteristics nozzle solver, validated within 0.03° of exact theory

Flight dynamics · Python + three.js

6-DOF Flight Simulator for a Liquid Sounding Rocket

Six-degree-of-freedom simulator written from scratch that sizes, flies and stress-tests a 30,000 ft rocket powered by the E5-75, with an interactive 3D replay in the browser.

  • Sized to 30,000 ft: 54 kg at liftoff, max Mach 1.70, 2.0 caliber static margin
  • 500-flight Monte Carlo: apogee 30,080 ± 2,330 ft, all requirements met
  • Fin flutter check caught undersized fins; redesign raised margin from 1.32 to 2.01

Rocket Navigation: Error-State Kalman Filter

A 25-state error-state EKF fuses a MEMS IMU, GPS and a barometer into position, velocity and attitude from the pad to apogee at Mach 1.7.

  • 1.8 m RMS position error and 0.30° pointing error at apogee
  • 99.7% of errors inside the filter's own 3-sigma bounds (NEES consistency test)
  • Rides through a 20 s GPS outage at burnout and recovers within 3 s

Spacecraft thermal · Python

Adaptive Spacecraft Radiator: Heater Power Study

First-order thermal model estimating how much heater power an adaptive-emissivity radiator coating saves over fixed coatings in low Earth orbit. Built from public data as an independent study.

  • Lumped radiator node with solar, albedo and Earth IR loads, solved to periodic steady state
  • Adaptive coating removed the cold-case heater: about 47 W and 1.6 kg saved versus OSR
  • Transition-temperature sweep and 500-run Monte Carlo on the savings
